
# CashCompass AI

### Your Intelligent Financial Navigation Assistant

CashCompass AI is an AI-powered financial forecasting and smart budgeting assistant designed to help individuals understand their spending, plan their budgets, and prepare for future financial needs.

## Problem Statement

Many people struggle with unplanned spending, poor financial forecasting, unexpected expenses, and limited personalized budgeting guidance.

Traditional expense trackers mainly show past spending rather than helping users prepare for future financial challenges.

## Proposed Solution

CashCompass AI aims to provide a simple financial dashboard that helps users:

- Track income and expenses.
- Understand spending patterns.
- Monitor monthly budgets.
- Forecast future cash flow.
- Identify potential cash shortages.
- Receive personalized budgeting and saving suggestions.

## Planned Features

1. Smart Expense Tracking
2. Monthly Budget Monitoring
3. Cash-Flow Forecasting
4. Financial Risk Alerts
5. Smart Saving Recommendations
6. What-If Financial Simulations

## Proposed Technology Stack

- Python
- Pandas
- Scikit-learn
- Streamlit
- Plotly
- SQLite

## Project Domain

Artificial Intelligence | Financial Technology (FinTech)

## Project Vision

To make personal financial planning simpler, smarter, and more proactive through technology.

**Navigate Money. Predict Tomorrow. Plan Better.**
